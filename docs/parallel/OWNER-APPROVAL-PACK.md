# Consolidated owner approval pack (single table)

Date: 2026-11-24 · Version 1 (Master draft; worker inputs listed in §4 are merged when delivered) · Status of every row: **UNDECIDED. Nothing in this document is an approval, an agreement, a legal opinion, a security attestation or a regional guarantee.** A row is ticked only with a reference (ticket, signed document, dated message) recorded in §5.

How to read it: **"Blocks"** says whether the item stops *development* (never, for anything below) or only *activation* of a live capability. All development proceeds meanwhile with mocks. Costs are upper bounds from existing repository documents and are **re-verified by S02/S03 against current official rates before you are asked to approve** (inputs pending, marked `TBC`). Sources: `docs/ai-intelligence/OWNER-APPROVAL-CHECKLIST.md`, `S-AG4-*`, `S-AG5-*`, `S-AG6-*`, `docs/parallel/OWNER-DECISIONS.md`, `docs/security/AI-ENGINE-SECURITY-FINDINGS.md`.
