## Other deviations recorded here
- **Migration numbers are 1000–1003**, not 900–999: `spec/db/ownership.yaml` (precedence 2) reserves M08 = `1000-1199` and M07 = `900-999`, and the migration runner rejects ordinals outside a schema owner's range.
- **ADR numbers 050–054** (not 018–022) for D-05..D-09: M05/M06/M07 sessions are creating ADRs from 018 on; 050+ avoids collisions.
- **Audit action names** have three parts (`AuditAction` type): `data.database.created|deleted|restored|rotated`, `data.database.restore_verified`, `data.bucket.created|policy_changed|made_public`, `data.signed_url.issued`, `email.domain.verified`, `email.mailbox.created|deleted`, `email.message.sent`, `email.suppression.added`, `data.provider.enabled` (prompt §9 names are the same without the leading `data.`/`email.` where it had two parts).
- **Endpoints not in docs/04 §2.8** (proposed additions PA-19..PA-21 appended to docs/04 §2.13; NOT implemented over HTTP): mailbox setup reveal, admin deliverability console/override, admin fleet read, second-person approval of in-place restore.
